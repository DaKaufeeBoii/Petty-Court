# Petty Court — Animated Film Script

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** Tiny household grievances receive wildly serious, funny justice.
- **Identity:** The product's paper-and-wine palette: aged parchment, oxblood, brass, and ink. Editorial serif display paired with a crisp grotesk and docket-style mono.
- **Hero:** Judge Reginald P. Pettiness, from the product's original vector portrait, presides over a growing paper courtroom.
- **Motion:** Evidence-card match cuts, a gavel-driven camera punch, and a verdict stamp that physically lands.
- **Arc:** A domestic micro-offense becomes an official case, the courtroom swells into ceremony, then the verdict collapses back to the original small nuisance.
- **Payoff:** End on a stamped docket seal whose tiny “PETTY” center becomes the opening's gavel head.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24 seconds, five scenes.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.00s / 4.00s — “THE CARTON.”
**Purpose:** Hook with the absurd gap between a tiny offense and courtroom gravity.

**Exact content:** 0.00–0.45: giant red-ink word “EXHIBIT”. 0.45–1.20: “A” snaps beside it. 1.25–2.40: evidence label “EMPTY MILK CARTON / RETURNED TO FRIDGE”. 2.40–3.35: “THE PEOPLE v. THE CARTON RETURNER”. 3.35–4.00: docket seal “PETTY COURT · NO. 0042”.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Start in a macro crop of a circular brass gavel head, as large as half the short edge, on parchment. A torn evidence tag sits at lower-left; a wine-red ink blot anchors upper-right. The physical gavel silhouette is an illustrated graphic object, not a product image. Paper fibers and engraved brass marks imply court archive ephemera. A hard ellipse of light catches the gavel.

**Choreography:** 0.00 gavel is already present, cropped in extreme foreground; a fine ring is visible behind it. 0.15 the gavel drops 6% and hits; the ring ripples outward. 0.35 “EXHIBIT” punches into upper-left, 0.48 “A” clicks into a brass square. 0.85 a tiny milk-carton silhouette flips onto the evidence label; a single drop falls, then the label receives a red underline. 1.65 a second paper strip is pulled from under the gavel, revealing the named parties. 2.25 camera punches toward the gavel's circular head; the text shears upward as the seal rotates 12 degrees. 3.25 seal expands and moves to center, becoming the exact incoming circular carrier.

**Transition contract:** The brass gavel head becomes the brass outer ring of Shot 02's court seal. Combine a short zoom-through, paper split opening, and red-ink type shear; preserve circle center and rotation.

**Assets and technique:** CSS/SVG gavel and milk carton illustration; paper grain via light CSS texture; masked type; animated SVG impact ripple. No supplied brand assets.

**Sound intent:** Instrumental funk / nu-disco at 108 BPM, playful but dignified, one clear hit near 0.15s and rising accents near scene changes. No VO or SFX requested. Beat alignment unverified pending bed.

### Shot 02 / 4.00–8.00s / 4.00s — “A MATTER OF STATE.”

**Exact content:** 4.15 “IN THE PEOPLE’S COURT OF PETTINESS”; 4.75 “A VERY SMALL CLAIM”; 5.45 left docket “PLAINTIFF / NEXT PERSON NEEDING MILK”; 6.15 right docket “DEFENDANT / THE CARTON RETURNER”; 6.90 “NO LAWYERS. NO CONSEQUENCES. ALL FEELINGS.”

**Composition and artistic idea:** A wide proscenium court emerges from two folded paper wings. Judge silhouette stays centered and small at first; plaintiff and defendant docket cards occupy the far left and right, creating a stage-like gulf. Oxblood curtain panels frame parchment center; thin brass rules give the paper depth.

**Choreography:** 0.00 incoming seal is centered and fills 58% of the short edge. 0.20 it splits along a vertical crease; left and right paper wings unfold in opposite directions. 0.55 the court title is revealed beneath the crease while the judge's silhouette rises from behind the bench. 1.30 plaintiff docket card stamps down from upper-left; 1.95 defendant card answers from upper-right. A brass line draws across the bench, then bends into a tiny balance scale. 3.05 both cards rotate toward the lens and fold together into one docket folio, which falls forward to fill the frame.

**Transition contract:** Preserve the docket folio's rectangular silhouette. Its paper fold becomes the statute page in Shot 03; combine fold-through perspective and an oxblood panel clipping diagonally across the page.

**Assets and technique:** Custom vector courtroom architecture, cards, gavel, judge silhouette; perspective transform; SVG rules and balance scale. Typography remains fully readable in safe frame.

**Sound intent:** Music continues; aim the fold impact at the next verified downbeat. Alignment unverified until the track is analyzed.

### Shot 03 / 8.00–13.00s / 5.00s — “THE LAW.”
**Purpose:** Show the app turns one-line disputes into playful, invented legal proceedings.

**Exact content:** 8.10 heading “THE CHARGE”; 8.55 “UNAUTHORIZED CARTON RE-ENTRY”; 9.35 statute citation “DOMESTIC SNACK ALLOCATION CODE · ART. XII”; 10.25 “THE EVIDENCE: ONE EMPTY CARTON”; 11.20 “THE DEFENSE: ‘THERE WAS PROBABLY ONE MORE DROP.’”; 12.20 “THE COURT HAS HEARD ENOUGH.”

**Composition and artistic idea:** Begin top-down on a legal page; a thick red rule and typewriter-style docket numbering run vertically. A brass scale arm occupies right third; paper text owns left two-thirds. A macro crop of a single milk drop becomes a circular magnifier over the center, making the microscopic evidence feel forensic.

**Choreography:** 0.00 folio opens under the lens. 0.25 charge types on in three bursts, the last word “RE-ENTRY” overprinting itself once. 1.20 citation is revealed by a roller-like brass rule tracking down the page. 2.10 magnifying lens swings in on a hinge; inside it, the single milk drop magnifies to 5x and trembles once. 3.10 defense quote is typed into a marginal note; a red “OBJECTION” mark is crossed out by a clean brass line. 4.15 page is pinched at the lower corner and lifted; its underside is a wine-red circular stamp plate. Camera dives into that circle.

**Transition contract:** The circular stamp plate expands as a radial aperture. Preserve the circle and red ink color; pair the aperture with type splitting vertically and a shallow depth punch into Shot 04.

**Assets and technique:** SVG page, scale, magnifier and drop; line tracing; monospace docket text; layered transforms (no large blur/filter plane).

**Sound intent:** Continue instrumental; a lighter ticking subdivision underscores type, then a strong impact near 13s if the track supports it. No added SFX.

### Shot 04 / 13.00–18.00s / 5.00s — “THE RULING.”
**Purpose:** Reveal the product's humorous judge and make verdict generation the emotional climax.

**Exact content:** 13.00 “THE HONORABLE”; 13.55 “REGINALD P. PETTINESS”; 14.20 “CHIEF JUDGE · VERY SMALL CLAIMS”; 14.85 brass stamp: “THE COURT / GUILTY / HAS RULED”; 16.10 “MUST RETURN THE CARTON WITH A CEREMONIAL APOLOGY.”; 17.10 “COURT IS ADJOURNED. THE GROUP CHAT MAY NOW RESUME.”

**Composition and artistic idea:** The original Judge Reginald vector portrait—recognizable burgundy robe, dark hair, warm face, gold details—is centered and large, occupying 74% of the short edge. Behind him, an oxblood disk sits on cream paper; an oversized verdict ring lands in front without obscuring his expression. The visual gag: judicial gravitas is enormous while the case remains about a carton.

**Choreography:** 0.00 aperture opens around the judge as he appears at 80% scale. 0.40 he rises to 100%, robe settles with one 2-degree recoil. 0.95 gold nameplate draws under the portrait. 1.75 stamp arm rotates down from top-right, briefly held just above the seal. 2.05 it slams, compresses the paper by 3%, rebounds, and leaves a red double-ring impression reading “GUILTY”. Tiny paper flecks pop only at the contact point. 3.10 sentence line appears within the lower margin, one line at a time. 4.15 portrait turns his eyes toward the sentence; frame pushes into the stamp ink until the double-ring fills the canvas.

**Transition contract:** Stamp's concentric ink rings swell to full frame and transform into a solid oxblood field. Combine ring expansion, paper-to-ink color conversion, and matching radial camera push into Shot 05.

**Assets and technique:** Judge portrait redrawn faithfully from the source's SVG silhouette; SVG stamp arm and rings; brass lighting; local font; radial mask. No photoreal object depiction required.

**Sound intent:** Music carries the verdict accent; no separate gavel SFX requested.

## Variety review

The sequence changes scale and medium across the adjacent shots: macro prop/evidence, wide proscenium, top-down legal page, centered portrait hero, typographic color-field finale. Cuts are carried by a circle, paper folio, radial stamp, and seal rather than repeated wipes. Motion progresses from sharp comedy beats to courtroom ceremony and a quieter typographic close. Every scene has a changing action in its final fifth to motivate the next cut; cut timing is provisional until audio onsets are measured.
